| **03** | `CC-03` | **Essential Terminology, Coordination Sphere & Oxidation State** | Central metal (Lewis acid), ligands (Lewis bases), coordination sphere vs ionization sphere, coordination number formula $\text{CN} = \sum (\text{ligands} \times \text{denticity})$, algebraic oxidation state rules, zero-valent [Ni(CO)₄], Fe(+I) in brown ring [Fe(H₂O)₅(NO)]SO₄. |
| **04** | `CC-04` | **Ligands: Complete Classification, Ambidentate & Flexidentate** | Monodentate, bidentate (en, ox, gly, dmg), polydentate (dien, trien, EDTA⁴⁻); ambidentate ligands (NO₂⁻ vs ONO⁻, SCN⁻ vs NCS⁻, CN⁻ vs NC⁻); flexidentate ligands (EDTA acting as pentadentate in [Co(EDTA)Br]²⁻). |
| **05** | `CC-05` | **Chelate Effect, Macrocyclic Effect & Stability Energetics** | 5- and 6-membered chelate rings, thermodynamic origin: entropy-driven ($\Delta S > 0$ due to release of coordinated water molecules), macrocyclic effect (porphyrin, crown ethers, pre-organization), applications in water hardness (EDTA) and antidote chelation therapy (Pb-EDTA, Wilson's disease). |
| **06** | `CC-06` | **IUPAC Nomenclature & Formula Standards** | Cation first, alphabetical ligand order (ammine double 'm', aqua, carbonyl, nitrosyl), anionic ligand suffix (-ido, -ito, -ato), bis/tris prefixes, anionic metal suffix '-ate' (ferrate, cuprate, platinate), oxidation state in Roman numerals, complex-cation complex-anion naming ([Pt(NH₃)₄][PtCl₄]). |
| **07** | `CC-07` | **Structural Isomerism: Ionization, Solvate, Linkage & Coordination** | Ionization ([Co(NH₃)₅SO₄]Br vs [Co(NH₃)₅Br]SO₄), solvate/hydrate ([Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ violet vs [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂·H₂O blue-green vs [Cr(H₂O)₄Cl₂]Cl·2H₂O dark green), linkage (nitro vs nitrito), coordination ([Co(NH₃)₆][Cr(CN)₆]), ligand & coordination-position isomerism. |
| **08** | `CC-08` | **Stereoisomerism: Geometrical (Cis-Trans & Fac-Mer) in CN = 4 and 6** | CN 4 tetrahedral has NO GI (all 109.5° adjacent); square planar [Ma₂b₂] (cisplatin vs transplatin), [Mabcd] (3 GI), [M(AB)₂]; CN 6 octahedral [Ma₄b₂] (cis, trans), [Ma₃b₃] (facial vs meridional), [M(AA)₂b₂]. *(Includes Fig. 8.1 Vector Geometry)* |
| **09** | `CC-09` | **Stereoisomerism: Optical Activity & Master Counting Tables** | Chirality conditions (no plane $\sigma$, no inversion center $i$), [M(AA)₃] chiral $\Delta/\Lambda$ enantiomers, [M(AA)₂b₂] cis is chiral / trans is achiral; master counting table: [Mabcdef] gives 15 GI and 30 stereoisomers (15 enantiomeric pairs). |
| **10** | `CC-10` | **Bonding Theories: Sidgwick's EAN Rule & Valence Bond Theory** | $\text{EAN} = Z - \text{OS} + 2(\text{CN})$, 36 (Kr) noble gas attainment; VBT orbital hybridization ($sp^3, dsp^2, d^2sp^3, sp^3d^2$), inner vs outer orbital complexes, low-spin vs high-spin, 5 worked cases ([Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻, [Fe(H₂O)₆]²⁺, [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺, [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻, [NiCl₄]²⁻), VBT limitations. |
| **11** | `CC-11` | **Crystal Field Theory (CFT): Octahedral Splitting & CFSE** | Electrostatic point charge model, lifting of degeneracy, $e_g$ ($d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}$) raised $+0.6 \Delta_o$, $t_{2g}$ ($d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx}$) lowered $-0.4 \Delta_o$, $\text{CFSE}_{oct}$ formula, weak field ($\Delta_o < P$, high spin) vs strong field ($\Delta_o > P$, low spin), $d^4\text{ to }d^7$ CFSE values. *(Includes Fig. 11.1 Vector Splitting)* |
| **12** | `CC-12` | **CFT: Tetrahedral Splitting, Spectrochemical Series & Factors on Δ** | Inverted splitting: $t_2$ raised $+0.4 \Delta_t$, $e$ lowered $-0.6 \Delta_t$, quantitative relation $\Delta_t = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_o$, why all tetrahedral complexes are high-spin ($\Delta_t < P$); spectrochemical series: $\text{I}^- < \dots < \text{H}_2\text{O} < \text{NH}_3 < \text{en} < \text{CN}^- < \text{CO}$; factors governing $\Delta$: oxidation state ($M^{3+} > M^{2+}$), series ($5d > 4d > 3d$). |
| **13** | `CC-13` | **CFT in Square Planar Complexes & Splitting Hierarchy** | $D_{4h}$ splitting: $d_{x^2-y^2} \gg d_{xy} > d_{z^2} > (d_{xz}=d_{yz})$, $\Delta_{sp} \approx 1.74 \Delta_o$, why $d^8$ metals prefer square planar ($d_{x^2-y^2}$ empty), [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ (square planar, diamag) vs [NiCl₄]²⁻ (tetrahedral, paramag), 4d/5d $d^8$ metals (Pd²⁺, Pt²⁺, Au³⁺) universally square planar. *(Includes Fig. 13.1 Vector Hierarchy)* |
| **14** | `CC-14` | **Jahn–Teller Distortion & Tetragonal Elongation** | Jahn–Teller theorem: non-linear degenerate system distorts to lower energy, strong distortion in asymmetric $e_g$ ($d^9$ in $\text{Cu}^{2+}$, HS $d^4$ in $\text{Cr}^{2+}$, LS $d^7$), weak distortion in asymmetric $t_{2g}$, zero distortion in $d^3, \text{HS } d^5, \text{LS } d^6, d^8, d^{10}$; tetragonal elongation (z-out): 4 short equatorial + 2 long axial bonds in $[\text{Cu(H}_2\text{O)}_6]^{2+}$. |
| **15** | `CC-15` | **Colour in Coordination Compounds & Absorption Spectroscopy** | $d\text{-}d$ transitions, complementary visible color wheel (absorbs red $\rightarrow$ green, absorbs violet $\rightarrow$ yellow), stepwise substitution $[\text{Ni(H}_2\text{O)}_6]^{2+}$ (green) to $[\text{Ni(en)}_3]^{2+}$ (violet), crystal field gemstones: Ruby ($\text{Cr}^{3+}$ in $\alpha\text{-Al}_2\text{O}_3$, red) vs Emerald ($\text{Cr}^{3+}$ in beryl, green), why anhydrous $\text{CuSO}_4$ is white ($\Delta = 0$). |
| **16** | `CC-16` | **Bonding in Metal Carbonyls: Synergic Effect & IR ν_CO Trends** | Synergic bonding: $\sigma$-donation ($\text{C}\rightarrow\text{M}$) + $\pi$-backdonation ($\text{M}(d\pi)\rightarrow\text{CO}(\pi^*)$), bond order trade-off: $\text{M-C}$ strengthens and shortens while $\text{C-O}$ weakens and lengthens; isoelectronic series $[\text{Mn(CO)}_6]^+ > [\text{Cr(CO)}_6] > [\text{V(CO)}_6]^-$: negative charge increases backdonation $\rightarrow$ lowest $\nu_{\text{CO}}$. *(Includes Fig. 16.1 Vector Mechanism)* |
| **17** | `CC-17` | **Thermodynamic vs Kinetic Stability & Irving–Williams Series** | Stepwise $K_n$ vs overall $\beta_n$ ($\log \beta_n = \sum \log K_n$), $K_1 > K_2 > \dots > K_n$ trend, Irving–Williams stability order: $\text{Ba}^{2+} < \dots < \text{Mn}^{2+} < \text{Fe}^{2+} < \text{Co}^{2+} < \text{Ni}^{2+} < \mathbf{\text{Cu}^{2+} > \text{Zn}^{2+}}$ (Jahn-Teller copper peak); thermodynamic stability ($\beta$) vs kinetic lability/inertness ($d^3 \text{ Cr}^{3+}$, LS $d^6 \text{ Co}^{3+}$ inert). |
| **18** | `CC-18` | **Applications of Coordination Compounds & JEE Advanced Master Sheet** | Analytical tests: $[\text{Ni(dmg)}_2]$ scarlet red ppt with O-H···O bonds; MacArthur-Forrest cyanide extraction of Au/Ag ($[\text{Au(CN)}_2]^-$ and Zn displacement); Mond nickel process ($[\text{Ni(CO)}_4]$); bio-inorganic: Chlorophyll ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$), Hemoglobin ($\text{Fe}^{2+}$), Vitamin B₁₂ ($\text{Co}^{3+}$); medicinal: Cisplatin anti-cancer, EDTA chelation therapy; JEE Reasoning Bank. |
