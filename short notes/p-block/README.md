# The p-Block Elements — Complete Explanatory Short Notes

A comprehensive, teacher-dense, visual handwritten-style explanatory short note set covering the entire **p-Block (Groups 13 to 18)** plus master comparative cross-group reviews for **JEE Main & JEE Advanced**.

Built strictly from **NCERT (Class 11 & 12)**, **Allen Classroom Modules**, and **JD Lee (Concise Inorganic Chemistry)**.

## 🎨 Visual Typography & Architecture
- **Navy Prose (`#0e2a47`):** Stepwise explanatory reasoning, mechanism pathways, and reaction conditions.
- **Teal Headings & Panels (`#00796b`):** Fundamental physical principles and *why* things occur.
- **Yellow Key Results (`#fef3c7` / `#92400e`):** Definitive formulas, trends, and results to memorise.
- **Red Warning Traps (`#fee2e2` / `#b91c1c`):** Common misconceptions, examiners' traps, and anomalous exceptions.
- **Vector Diagrams & Figures:** Handcrafted vector structures for Diborane banana bonds, Borax anion ring, Silicate polyhedra, P4 & Red P, P4O10 adamantane cage, S8 crown ring, and Xenon fluoride geometries.
