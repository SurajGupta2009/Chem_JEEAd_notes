PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS — Redox Reactions & Equivalent Concept
============================================================

1. Paper Format:
   - Size: Standard A4 (210 x 297 mm / 8.27 x 11.69 inches)
   - Orientation: Portrait
   - Safe Margin: ~10 mm on all edges (built into the layouts)

2. Scaling & Printer Driver Settings:
   - Scaling Mode: Select "FIT TO PRINTABLE AREA" or "FIT" (100% scale).
   - DO NOT select "Fill Entire Page", "Crop", or "Stretch", as this will clip margins.
   - Quality: High Quality / Best / 300 DPI or 600 DPI.

3. Duplex (Double-Sided) Printing:
   - Flip Option: "Flip on Long Edge" (Long-Edge Binding).
   - Paper Recommendation: 80 GSM or 100 GSM Bond/Bright White paper.

4. Source Formats Available:
   - PDF: pdf/redox-complete.pdf (Vector text + vector chemical structures).
   - PNG: png/RD_p01.png to RD_p19.png (Full 2480 x 3508 px raster images at nominal 300 DPI).
