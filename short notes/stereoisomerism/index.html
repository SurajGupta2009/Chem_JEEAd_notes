What is inside

Part I (29 sheets) — the isomerism map and the vocabulary; geometrical isomerism (the two conditions, C=N centres and the oxime family, E/Z and CIP, rings, cumulenes, spiro, bicyclics, counting, physical properties); optical isomerism (the polarimeter and specific rotation, chirality and the σ/i/Sn test, meso and racemic, resolution, projections, R/S, D/L, threo/erythro, chirality without a stereocentre, counting, relation questions); conformational analysis (barriers, ethane and butane, cyclohexane, substituted cyclohexanes); reaction stereochemistry (SN1/SN2, E2, syn/anti addition, hydrogenation); the C5H11Cl enumeration; twenty traps; and a one-page recall sheet.

Part II (12 sheets) — symmetry rigorously (five operations, the S1 = σ and S2 = i identities, point groups of ethane conformers, a chirality decision tree), axial chirality, ring junctions, trans-cycloalkenes and Bredt's rule, quantitative conformational analysis (A-values, 1,3-diaxial interactions, the anomeric effect), the ring-flip path, stereospecific versus stereoselective reactions and the Cram/Felkin–Anh models, enumeration by orbit counting (Burnside), topicity and pseudoasymmetry, ee and chiroptical methods, eight solved Advanced problems, applications, and a final audit.

SCOPE.md maps every lecture of the source to a sheet and classifies each sheet as core / advanced / support. SOURCE-AUDIT.md records the conflicts found inside the source, the corrections made, and exactly what was and was not verified.
