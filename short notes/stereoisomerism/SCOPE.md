# Scope and classification

Two questions this file answers: **what is in each sheet, and why**, and **what was
deliberately left out**. The chemistry chapter stops at the JEE Advanced edge; there is no
Olympiad section.

## 1. Level labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CORE** | JEE Main + Advanced core; a student cannot skip it. |
| **ADV** | JEE Advanced level: uses core ideas but asks a question the core sheets do not. |
| **SUPPORT** | Method, trap list or recall material that shortens the other sheets. |
| **EDGE** | Inside the Advanced syllabus but outside what the source covers; included because Advanced papers have used it. |

Prerequisites are named per sheet. The whole chapter assumes only: hybridisation and
VSEPR (sp, sp², sp³, 120°/109.5°), σ/π bonding and restricted rotation about π bonds, the
idea of a potential-energy diagram, and ΔG = −RT ln K. No orbital-symmetry theory, no
kinetics beyond “rate depends on concentration”, no spectroscopy beyond “light has a
wavelength and a polarisation plane”.

## 4. Deliberately out of scope

* **No Olympiad material.** Per the project rule for chemistry, Part II stops at JEE
  Advanced. Excluded on that ground: asymmetric catalysis and ligand design, Woodward–
  Hoffmann/pericyclic stereochemistry, stereochemistry of sigmatropic rearrangements,
  exciton chirality and advanced chiroptical theory, conformational analysis by molecular
  mechanics, and stereochemical databases.
* **No reaction catalogue.** Named reactions appear only where the stereochemical
  outcome is the point (hydroboration, hydrogenation, S<sub>N</sub>2, E2, Grignard
  additions); there is no “list of reactions of alkenes”.
* **No organic spectroscopy chapter.** ORD/CD appear in X-09 only as *evidence for
  configuration*, not as a spectroscopy topic.
* **No inorganic or physical stereochemistry chapters** (coordination geometry beyond the
  Λ/Δ mention in X-11, crystal packing, chirality of surfaces).
* **Polymer tacticity** is one paragraph in X-11, because JEE asks the definition, not
  the mechanism.
