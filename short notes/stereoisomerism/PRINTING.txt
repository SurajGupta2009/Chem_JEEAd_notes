STEREOSOMERISM — SHORT NOTES · PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS
====================================================

Paper size .......... A4 PORTRAIT (210 x 297 mm). The files are already A4; nothing
                      needs to be resized.
Sheets per page ..... ONE. Do not print 2-up, 4-up or booklet. The handwriting is set
                      at roughly 11 pt and the tables need the full width.
Scaling ............. "Fit" (fit to printable area). Do NOT choose "Fill" and do NOT
                      choose "Crop". "Actual size / 100%" is also acceptable on printers
                      whose unprintable margin is under 10 mm.
Margins ............. About 10 mm of safe area is built into every page. If the printer
                      needs more, choose "Fit"; do not shift or crop the page.
Duplex .............. Optional. If you duplex, use "flip on long edge" so that page 2 of a
                      sheet sits behind page 1.
Colour .............. Needed. Navy prose, teal headings, yellow key results and red
                      warnings all carry meaning. On a mono printer the yellow boxes turn
                      light grey; the text is still readable but the colour coding is lost.
Background .......... Print backgrounds ON (the paper tone and the faint ruling are part
                      of the look). If backgrounds are off, everything still reads, in pure
                      white.

WHICH FILE TO PRINT
-------------------
pdf/stereoisomerism-complete.pdf ....... Part I then Part II, one bookmark per sheet.
                                        Total 67 pages.
pdf/stereoisomerism-jee.pdf ............ Part I only, 51 pages (J-01 … J-29).
pdf/stereoisomerism-extensions.pdf ..... Part II only, 16 pages (X-01 … X-12).

The PNGs in jee/ and extensions/ are byte-for-byte the same pages at 2480 x 3508 px,
nominal 300 dpi. Print them at "Fit" with backgrounds on. The ZIPs in downloads/ are just
those PNGs collected, plus the PDFs in the complete ZIP.

READING ORDER
-------------
Part I is written to be read straight through: J-01 (cover and legend), J-02 (the map),
then J-03 … J-09 geometrical, J-10 … J-20 optical, J-21 … J-24 conformational,
J-25 … J-27 reaction stereochemistry and enumeration, J-28 traps, J-29 recall.

Part II is not a second textbook: each sheet assumes the core sheet named in its first
paragraph. Read X-01 after J-12, X-02 after J-18, X-03 after J-07, X-04/X-05 after J-23,
X-06 after J-26, X-07 after J-19, X-08 after J-19, X-09 after J-14, and X-10 … X-12 last.

BINDING
-------
A single staple in the top-left corner or a bulldog clip is enough; the pages are meant to
be shuffled and re-sorted by sheet id. If you bind, put Part I first — the combined PDF is
already in that order.

If something looks wrong on paper (a clipped table, a missing page), check the same page in
the PDF first. The PDF and the PNGs come from the same build, so a difference between them
is a printer setting, not a missing page.
