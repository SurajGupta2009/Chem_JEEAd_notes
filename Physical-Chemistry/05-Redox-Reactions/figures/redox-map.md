## Three tiers (§1–§4)
- Classical: gain or loss of O / H (§1)
  - fails for 2Na + Cl₂; CaO + SO₃ is not redox
- Electronic: loss or gain of e⁻ (§2)
  - OIL RIG; the oxidant is reduced
  - competitive transfer → activity series (§3)
- Oxidation number (§4)
  - rules in priority order: F −1 › Group 1/2 › H › O › sum = charge
  - NCl₃: N −3, Cl +1 (hydrolysis evidence)
- Electron density (NCERT 7.3.4, §13)

## Hard species (§5–§6)
- Peroxide O–O
  - H₂SO₅, H₂S₂O₈: S +6
  - CrO₅: Cr +6
- Inequivalent atoms
  - Na₂S₂O₃: −2, +6
  - S₄O₆²⁻: 0, +5 (avg +2.5)
  - NH₄NO₃: −3, +5
  - Fe₃O₄, Pb₃O₄: avg +8⁄3
- Odd ones
  - KO₂ −½ · O₂F₂ +1 · OF₂ +2
  - brown ring: Fe +1, NO⁺
  - carbonyls: 0 or negative metal

## Classify (§7–§8)
- Combination (needs an element)
- Decomposition (element among products)
- Displacement
  - metal: thermite, Kroll
  - non-metal: H₂ from water/acid, halogens
- Disproportionation
  - P₄, S₈, Cl₂ in OH⁻
  - MnO₄²⁻, Mn³⁺, Cu⁺, NO₂
  - cannot: F₂, ClO₄⁻
- Comproportionation 🆇
  - IO₃⁻ + I⁻ · Mn²⁺ + MnO₄⁻ · bleach + acid
- Role from O.S. range
  - maximum → oxidant only (HNO₃)
  - minimum → reductant only (H₂S)
  - middle → both (SO₂, H₂O₂)

## Balance (§9–§11)
- O.N. method: molecular equations
- Ion–electron: ionic, basic, disproportionation
- Medium changes the ratio
  - MnO₄⁻ : I⁻ = 1 : 5 acid, 1 : 3 base
- n-factor
  - MnO₄⁻ 5 / 3 / 1
  - Cr₂O₇²⁻ 6 · C₂O₄²⁻ 2 · FeC₂O₄ 3
  - S₂O₃²⁻ 1 (I₂) or 8 (Cl₂)

## Titrate (§12)
- KMnO₄: self-indicator, dil. H₂SO₄, warm oxalate
- K₂Cr₂O₇: primary standard, diphenylamine, OK in HCl
- Iodometry: oxidant + KI → I₂ → S₂O₃²⁻
- Iodimetry: titrate with I₂

## Electrode processes (§14–§16)
- Daniell cell: anode = oxidation
- Salt bridge: circuit, neutrality, no mixing
- E° table: higher = stronger oxidant
- E°cell > 0 ⇔ spontaneous

## Oxidation-state map (§17–§18) 🆇
- Latimer test: E°right > E°left → disproportionates
  - Cl₂: stable in acid, disproportionates in base
  - combine links with n-weighted E°
- Frost diagram: lowest point = most stable
- p-block
  - Group 13: +3 → +1 (Tl⁺)
  - Group 14: +4 → +2 (Pb²⁺); PbO₂ oxidant
  - Group 15: +5 → +3 (Bi³⁺); NaBiO₃ oxidant
  - Group 16: +6 → +4; H₂SeO₄ > H₂SO₄
  - Group 17: per-halate power Br > I > Cl
  - Period 2: N max 4 bonds, O max +2, F never positive
